My Fridge

This page allows you to add an ingredient name, quantity in 3 different units: kilograms, grams and numbers of units, choose its category between Fruit & veg, Meat & fish, Dairy & eggs, Store cupboard, Frozen, and Other, as well as expiry date (latest today).

After adding an ingredient, it is displayed in its chosen category. No category selected displays as Other. The page displays how many ingredients have been added. After refreshing the page, the ingredients are saved in local storage so they are still there after the page is refreshed.

You can add and remove as many ingredients as you'd like. You also have the option of removing all ingredients from the page at once.

Lastly, you are able to search for recipes using that specific ingredient by clicking on "Search recipes".

Recipes

This page makes it possible to perform various functions. If navigated from the CTA in the header, it takes you to the main Recipes page with the search empty and all categories displayed. Once a category is selected, a list of meals within that category appear.

If navigated through the My Fridge page with a specific ingredient, it will only show recipes with the specific ingredient. You are able to clear search to keep searching for new ingredients.

Once a specific meal is clicked, it will take you to a meal page, where you can find the meal's name, photo, recipe video (if it exists), list of ingredients and the recipe information. You can also save the recipe as PDF.

The "Are you feeling lucky?" addition allows you to search for an ingredient and navigate to a random recipe containing this ingredient.
