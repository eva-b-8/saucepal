About Saucepal

Cook with what you have in your fridge.

Saucepal helps you keep track of what's in your fridge and find recipes that use it.

How it works

Add ingredients to your fridge, then search for recipes that use them. Your fridge is saved in your browser, so it's still there next time. Search for meals with your added ingredients.

You can also search for specific meals by searching on the Recipes page, as well as navigating through the categories.

Clicking inside a meal will navigate you to the recipe page, where you can find the meal's photo, name, recipe video, necessary ingredients and cooking instructions.

You can also download the recipe as a PDF to use offline.

This web app is available in light and dark modes and is built using a mobile-first approach.
